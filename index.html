📖 Course Overview

This course provides a rigorous foundation in the mathematics of computation, covering fundamental topics in logic, computability theory, complexity theory, and graph theory. Students will develop skills in formal reasoning, proof techniques, and algorithmic analysis essential for advanced computer science.

Course Progress: Lecture 1 of 28 completed (3.5%)

🎯 Learning Objectives

🧮 Logic & Proofs

  • Master propositional and predicate logic
  • Apply formal proof techniques
  • Understand soundness and completeness

💻 Computation Theory

  • Understand Turing machines and variants
  • Analyze decidability and undecidability
  • Explore the Church-Turing thesis

⚡ Complexity Theory

  • Classify problems by complexity
  • Master NP-completeness concepts
  • Apply polynomial-time reductions

🔗 Graph Theory

  • Analyze graph structures and algorithms
  • Understand spectral graph properties
  • Apply probabilistic graph methods
